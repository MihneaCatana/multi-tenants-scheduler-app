## Root Cause

Migrations 0006 and 0007 were **hand-authored** — no snapshot files, and migration 0007's timestamp (`1721462400000` = Jul 2024) is *older* than 0006's (`1752864000000` = Jan 2025). Drizzle orders by timestamp, so it treats 0007 as already-applied and skips it. The `__drizzle_migrations` tracker never gets created/updated properly. This is why "up to date" but only 6 migrations.
